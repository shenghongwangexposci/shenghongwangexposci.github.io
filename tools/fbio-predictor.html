Oral bioavailability (Fbio) model

A physiologically based toxicokinetic (PBTK) model predicting the fraction of an oral dose that survives presystemic ("first-pass") metabolism in the gut lumen, gut wall, and liver to reach systemic circulation — following the mechanistic model in Wang, Zhang, Saunders, Li & Li, Environmental Science & Technology 58 (2024) 14135–14145.

Model

A 3-compartment PBTK model (gut lumen × 3 segments, gut wall, liver, rest-of-body) integrated over the simulated time window. Permeability-based absorption (IVIVE from Papp,Caco-2) and clearance-based metabolism (IVIVE from in vitro intrinsic clearances) run concurrently as the dose transits the gut. Full derivation in the paper's SI, Text S1–S2.

Model validated against di-2-ethylhexyl phthalate (DEHP) and omeprazole, per the source paper's case studies. Source: Wang, S., Zhang, Z., Saunders, L. J., Li, D., Li, L. (2024). Understanding the impacts of presystemic metabolism on the human oral bioavailability of chemicals. doi.org/10.1021/acs.est.4c03344
