Screening-level calculators

Tools

Interactive implementations of published models, built for quick screening-level estimates in chemical risk assessment. Each one links back to the paper it's based on.

Fabs Predictor: GI absorption model

Predicts oral fraction absorbed (Fabs) in the human GI tract from octanol–water partitioning (KOW) and Caco-2 apparent permeability (Papp,Caco-2). Single-chemical slider mode plus batch mode for pasting a chemical list and exporting results as CSV.

Open tool →

Based on Wang, S., Zhang, Z., Li, D., & Li, L. (2024). Environment International, 193, 109108. doi.org/10.1016/j.envint.2024.109108

Fbio Predictor: oral bioavailability PBTK model

A physiologically based toxicokinetic model predicting Fbio, the fraction of an oral dose that survives presystemic ("first-pass") metabolism in the gut lumen, gut wall, and liver to reach systemic circulation. Includes a mass-flow diagram and validated DEHP / omeprazole examples.

Open tool →

Based on Wang, S., Zhang, Z., Saunders, L. J., Li, D., & Li, L. (2024). Environmental Science & Technology, 58(32), 14135–14145. doi.org/10.1021/acs.est.4c03344

Half-life Predictor: whole-body elimination model

Estimates a chemical's elimination half-life with the classical one-compartment relationship t½ = ln(2)·Vd/CLtotal, built on the same partitioning and IVIVE machinery as the Fbio tool — accounting for hepatic metabolism, optional gut-wall metabolism, and renal excretion.

Open tool →

Derived extension built on the PBTK model from Wang, S., Zhang, Z., Saunders, L. J., Li, D., & Li, L. (2024). Environmental Science & Technology, 58(32), 14135–14145 — half-life itself is not a value the paper reports.

Numerical ODE Solver

A general-purpose differential equation solver — enter any system of first-order ODEs and integrate it with Euler, Heun (RK2), or classical RK4. Includes presets for a 3-compartment PBTK model, exponential decay, logistic growth, an SIR epidemic model, Lotka–Volterra predator–prey dynamics, a damped oscillator, and the Van der Pol equation, plus chart output and CSV export.

Open tool →

General numerical methods utility — not tied to a specific publication.
